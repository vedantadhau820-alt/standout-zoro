Getting Started

How to use StandOut

A simple guide to help you understand the system, track your progress, and make the most out of your goals.

01

Why Use StandOut?

Every human has goals in life. We all want to achieve something, but achieving those goals requires consistent hard work.

The problem is, our brain struggles to stay motivated when it cannot track progress. Think about your goal — can you actually measure how close you are to achieving it? Most probably, the answer is no.

When the brain sees no visible progress, it starts to feel stuck. And when it feels stuck, it naturally looks for a way to escape from the task.

02

How StandOut Works For Your Brain

The app is designed to help you work towards your goals and stay consistent.

It tracks your progress and hard work, such as how many tasks you have completed. You also get rewarded for completing tasks with Improvement Points that can be used to buy Cards.

Music plays when you achieve something, while Momentum helps you stay consistent and Seasons encourage you to complete tasks every day.

Every feature is designed to help you stay motivated and reach your goals.

03

The First Rule of StandOut

This app is designed to show your progress, motivate you, and keep your eyes on your goals.

But none of this will work if you are not honest with yourself.

Don’t complete tasks just for the sake of earning Improvement Points or buying Cards. The real goal is to improve yourself and move closer to your goals.

04

How To Set Up The App

Step 1 — Goals

One of the most important things is Goals.

Set at least one high-priority, one medium-priority, and one low-priority goal.

Your low-priority goals should help you achieve your medium-priority goals, and your medium-priority goals should help you achieve your high-priority goal.

Goals give you direction — they show you where you need to go.

Step 2 — Skillset

Once you have your goals set up, add the skills you need to achieve them to your Skillset.

Your skills show how much experience you have in each one. These skills will help you decide what daily tasks you should work on.

Step 3 — Tasks

Every day, you can complete as many tasks as you want, but for quality productivity, you will earn Improvement Points for only 10 tasks per day.

There are three main ways to create tasks:

Goal-Oriented Add a task for each skill you have. For example, if your goal is to get a job and your skills are Python, English, and Aptitude, you can add tasks like Learn OOPs in Python or Practice English.
Health-Oriented Add tasks that improve your health, such as walking 1,000 steps, drinking 2L of water, or avoiding junk food.
Character-Oriented Add tasks that help you grow as a person, such as reading a book or watching a podcast.
Task Terminology

A task can be linked to a Skill, which helps you track your experience in that particular skill. This means every task you complete helps you get better.

You can also add a deadline. If you miss the deadline, your Improvement Points can be reduced, encouraging you to complete tasks on time.

You can set tasks to repeat. For example, a “Read 10 Pages” task can repeat daily instead of being added manually every day.

There is also Hardcore Mode. When enabled, missing that task will reduce your Improvement Points to 0. Use it for important, non-negotiable tasks.

Step 4 — Time & Timer

In the Time page, you can track how much time is left until a particular date and time.

The clock icon in the navigation bar opens the Timer. You can use it while writing an essay, cleaning, studying, or working on any other activity.

You can also play music from your saved music or your own music folder.

Step 5 — Customize Your App

In the Account page, you can change the music that plays when you complete a mission, unlock achievements, and more.

You can also add custom Cards and set your own app background to customize the experience.

Step 6 — Improvement Points

For every task you complete, you earn 1 Improvement Point.

These points can be used to buy Cards from the Marketplace, the first icon in the navigation bar.

The Cards you purchase can serve as rewards for your hard work and progress.

Step 7 — Track Your Progress

In the Skillset page, you can see your experience in each skill.

In the Account page, you can view your achievements.

In the Marketplace, you can see the Cards you have purchased.

Through the Graph icon in the navigation bar, you can view your monthly summary and track your overall progress.

Step 8 — Explore

We also have Daily Challenges that give you Improvement Points, Mastery to track your personal progress in any topic, and Seasons to motivate you to complete tasks every day.

Your Streak, also known as Momentum, helps you stay consistent.

There are also Monthly Resolutions and Monthly Badges, which you can find by clicking the app icon.

Explore the app to discover more.

One last thing. Be honest with yourself and use the app for your own growth. Don’t take things for granted or complete tasks just for the sake of it. Do them because they will help you improve and achieve your goals.
